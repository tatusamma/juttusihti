# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1. The full text is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

## In short

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and
publishing other people's private information are not accepted in issues, pull requests,
discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer,
[@tero-k](https://github.com/tero-k), through a
[private report](https://github.com/tero-k/juttusihti/security/advisories/new) marked as a
conduct issue. All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may
ban a participant temporarily or permanently.
